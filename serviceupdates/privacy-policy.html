Privacy Policy

What Service Updates does with your data — in short, your choices stay on your phone, and alerts reach you without us knowing who you are.

The short version: there are no accounts and no analytics. Your train companies, stations and alert settings are stored only on your phone. To send alerts, the app uses Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging, which gives your phone a random push token and signs it up to alerts for the train companies and days you choose. We send each alert to everyone following that train company; we never see or store tokens, devices or who follows what.
01

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your choices: the train companies and stations you pick, the days you want alerts, whether to alert only about your stations, and whether alerts are paused.
  • Alert history: which disruptions you’ve already been alerted about, so the same one isn’t sent twice and you only hear it has cleared if you were told it started. Entries older than a week are discarded.
  • A copy of the disruption list, so the app can show something when you're offline.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. Android's automatic backup is switched off for this app, so none of it is copied to your Google account.

02

Alerts and Firebase Cloud Messaging

Alerts are delivered by Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), a Google service. When you install the app, Firebase gives it a random push token and installation ID. These identify the app on your phone so Google can deliver messages; they aren't linked to your name, email or Google account by us.

The app then subscribes that token to message topics named after each train company you choose and each day you want alerts — for example sw-mon for South Western Railway on Mondays — plus a topic for network-wide disruption. Google stores these subscriptions to deliver messages. Your stations are not sent anywhere: the app checks them on your phone when an alert arrives.

Our server checks National Rail's disruption information every five minutes and, when something changes, sends one message to the topic for that train company and day. It doesn't know which phones, or how many, follow a topic, and it doesn't keep any list of devices. Google's handling of this data is covered by the Firebase privacy information.

The app re-checks its subscriptions when you change your settings and about once a day in the background. Every alert is shown or skipped by the app on your phone, based on your settings.

03

What else the app connects to

To show disruption in the app, it downloads a public list of current disruption from Google Cloud Storage (storage.googleapis.com). This is the same file for everyone and the request doesn't include your choices. Like any web request it carries your IP address, which Google uses to deliver the file; we don't keep access logs for it.

The More on National Rail button opens the National Rail website in your browser. Anything you do there is between you and National Rail.

The list of stations is built into the app, so searching for a station doesn't send anything.

04

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show disruption alerts. You can turn them off at any time in Android's settings, or pause them in the app.
  • Internet access: to download disruption information and receive alerts.

The app doesn't use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

05

What we don't do

  • No account, sign-up, or login of any kind
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or advertising
  • No location tracking
  • Nothing shared with or sold to anyone, beyond Google delivering alerts and the disruption list as described above
06

Data retention & deletion

Everything the app stores stays on your phone until you change it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall it. When you remove the app, its push token stops working and Google stops delivering to it. There's nothing held by us that identifies you, so there's nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

07

Where the information comes from

Disruption information is from National Rail Enquiries, provided by Rail Delivery Group through the Rail Data Marketplace. Station names contain public sector information from NaPTAN, licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Information can be incomplete, delayed or change quickly, so always check before you travel.

08

Children's privacy

Service Updates isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age.
