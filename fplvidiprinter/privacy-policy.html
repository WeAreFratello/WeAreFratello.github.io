Privacy Policy

What FPL Vidiprinter does with your data — in short, it stays on your phone, and the app talks only to the official FPL service.

The short version: we don't run a server, don't have accounts, and don't collect analytics. The app stores your FPL Team IDs, any friends' teams you add, and your settings on your phone. The only place it connects to is the official Fantasy Premier League service, to fetch public game data.
01

What the app stores

  • Your Team IDs for Classic and/or Draft. This is the same public number shown in your team's URL on the official FPL site.
  • Friends you add: the public Team ID of each team you choose to follow, and any nickname you give it.
  • Your settings: which game modes are on, your notification choices, your colour scheme, the default tab, and each home-screen widget's game mode.
  • Notification bookkeeping: the last Draft waiver and head-to-head result you were told about, so the same alert isn't sent twice.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps can't read. The live event feed and points totals are held in memory only and are cleared when the app's process ends.

Android's automatic backup is switched on for this app. If backup is enabled on your phone, Android may include these settings in your device backup in your own Google account, and restore them when you set up a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your account. We can't see or access it, and you can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.

02

What the app connects to

The app connects only to the official Fantasy Premier League API (fantasy.premierleague.com and, for Draft, draft.premierleague.com). These are the same public endpoints the official site uses. Using your Team IDs and your friends' Team IDs, it fetches player and fixture data, squads, live scores, league tables, Draft waivers, head-to-head results and the Team of the Week.

While matches are live, it checks for updates about every 30 seconds. If Draft is on, it also checks about every 30 minutes in the background for processed waivers and settled head-to-head results.

These requests go straight from your phone to the Premier League. Like any web request, they include your IP address and the Team IDs being looked up. The Premier League's own privacy policy covers what it does with them. Nothing passes through us, and we don't log or see any of it.

The Pick team, Transfers buttons, and the links for finding your Team ID, open the official FPL website in your browser. Anything you do there, including logging in, is between you and the Premier League. The app never sees your FPL password.

03

Notifications, alarms & background work

Every notification is generated on your phone from data it has already fetched. None of them pass through a push or messaging service of ours.

  • Live status: an ongoing notification while matches are being tracked. Android requires this to keep live updates running.
  • Match events: goals, assists, cards, bonus points and similar for your players, plus Draft waiver results and head-to-head results.
  • Deadline reminders: a heads-up one hour before each gameweek's transfer deadline, and before Draft waivers close.

The Alarms & reminders permission is used only to restart live tracking at the next kickoff. You can turn match events and deadline reminders off in the app's settings, and turn any notification off in Android's settings.

04

What we don't do

  • No account, sign-up, or login of any kind
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs
  • No advertising, and nothing shared or sold to third parties
  • No data stored on any server — we don't have one
05

Data retention & deletion

Everything the app stores stays on your phone until you change or remove it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall it. Uninstalling removes it from your phone. A copy may remain in your Google account's device backup until you delete that backup or Google expires it. There's nothing held by us to delete on your behalf.

06

Children's privacy

FPL Vidiprinter isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age.
