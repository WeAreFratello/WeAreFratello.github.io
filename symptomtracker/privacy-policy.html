Privacy Policy

What Symptom Tracker does with your health data — in short, it never leaves your phone unless you export it yourself.

The short version: everything you log is stored only on your device. The app has no internet permission, so Android itself prevents it from sending anything anywhere. There's no server, no account and no analytics. Data leaves your phone only when you choose to export or share a file.
01

What the app stores

Everything you enter: symptoms and their severity, food and drink, medication doses, bowel movements (Bristol scale), notes, and your own catalogue of items. Optionally, copies of the Health Connect data described below, and sleep estimates the app works out on the device.

All of it is kept in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps can't read. Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for this app, so it isn't copied to your Google account either.

02

Permissions and why they're used

  • Health Connect (read only): sleep, exercise, weight, heart-rate variability, mindfulness sessions. Read from other apps that have recorded them, so they can sit alongside what you log. The app never writes to or deletes anything in Health Connect, and only reads while it's open.
  • Usage access (optional). Used only to read when your screen turned on and off overnight, to estimate sleep on nights Health Connect has none. It never reads which apps you use. Only the estimated sleep time is stored.
  • Camera (optional). Used to photograph a food label so its ingredients can be read by on-device text recognition. The photo is held in memory only while it's read, then discarded. It's never saved to your phone or anywhere else.
  • Biometrics (optional). For the app lock, using Android's own fingerprint, face or screen-lock prompt. The app never sees or stores your fingerprint, face or PIN.
03

What leaves your phone

Nothing, unless you send it. The app can't connect to the internet: it doesn't have the permission, so Android blocks any attempt.

When you use Export, the app creates a file (a Markdown summary, or a JSON or CSV backup) and hands it to you through Android's save or share screen. Where it goes from there is your choice. The Markdown export contains no name, email or device identifier, but symptom, medication and note details can still be sensitive, so review a file before sharing it.

04

What we don't do

  • No account, sign-up or login of any kind
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking
  • No data stored on any server — we don't have one
  • Nothing shared with or sold to anyone
05

Third-party components

Food-label reading uses Google's ML Kit text recognition, which runs entirely on the device with a model built into the app. ML Kit includes a Google component that would normally send anonymous usage statistics about the library itself. Because Symptom Tracker has no internet permission, that component cannot send anything, and any such statistics stay queued on your device.

Health Connect is Google's on-device health data service. Symptom Tracker only reads from it with the permissions you grant, and you can change those at any time in Health Connect's settings.

06

Data retention & deletion

Your data stays on your phone until you delete entries in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall it. Uninstalling removes it completely. There's nothing held elsewhere for us to delete. Revoking Health Connect or Usage access stops new reads. Anything already copied stays in the app until you delete it.

07

Not medical advice

Symptom Tracker is a personal log. It doesn't diagnose, treat or give medical advice.

08

Children's privacy

Symptom Tracker isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age.
